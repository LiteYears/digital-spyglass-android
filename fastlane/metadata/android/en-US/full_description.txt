Ghost is a privacy-first Tor onion search aggregator for Android. It queries 13 public onion search engines in parallel, deduplicates and ranks the results, and opens links externally in Tor Browser.

KEY FEATURES

• 13 onion search engines queried in parallel
• RAM-only session — no queries, results, or history written to disk
• Three purge triggers: panic wipe, screen lock, app exit
• VPN gate — non-dismissible, blocks queries unless Orbot VPN is active
• Tor SOCKS5 transport with remote DNS via Tor
• FLAG_SECURE — blocks screenshots and recents thumbnails
• No backend, no telemetry, no accounts, no ads
• Stop button to cancel running searches
• Consent gate with 18+ verification, shown every session
• External link handoff to Tor Browser

PRIVACY

No data collected, stored, or transmitted to any server. Queries and results exist only in RAM during the active session. Only three non-sensitive preferences persist across sessions: engine selection, snippet filter, VPN requirement.

The app never renders remote HTML. Links open externally in Tor Browser via intent. Screenshots and recent-app previews are blocked.

REQUIREMENTS

• Android 7.0+ (API 24)
• Orbot in VPN mode (for Tor transport)
• Tor Browser for Android (for opening result links)

HOW IT WORKS

1. Enter a search query
2. Ghost queries 13 onion search engines in parallel via Tor
3. Results are parsed, deduplicated, and ranked
4. Tap any result to open it in Tor Browser
5. When you lock the screen, exit the app, or hit panic — all session data is wiped from RAM

WHO IS THIS FOR

Ghost is built for researchers, journalists, and people who need access to information in restrictive environments. It is a tool for legitimate open-source intelligence (OSINT), investigative journalism, academic study, and censorship circumvention. The app is designed for adults who take personal responsibility for their research activities.

The developer maintains a portfolio of OSINT and security research tools. Ghost is the mobile extension of this research toolchain.

USE CASES

• Journalism and investigative research in restrictive environments
• Security research and OSINT analysis
• Academic study of onion services and censorship circumvention
• Access to information in regions with restricted internet
• Privacy education and digital rights research
