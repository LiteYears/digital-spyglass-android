TARGET AUDIENCE STATEMENT (for Google Play Console — App Content > Target Audience)

Primary audience: Adults (18+) working in research, journalism, and security.

Target groups:
1. Journalists and investigative researchers who need access to information in restrictive environments where internet access is censored or monitored.
2. Security researchers and OSINT (open-source intelligence) analysts who study onion services, threat actors, and information patterns.
3. Academics studying censorship circumvention, anonymous communication, and digital rights.
4. People in regions with restricted internet access who use Tor for legitimate information access.

Age group: 18+ only. The app requires explicit adult consent at every launch via a non-dismissible consent gate.

Why this audience: The developer maintains a public portfolio of OSINT research tools (github.com/sookoothaii), including a spatial intelligence workstation with 53 live feeds and a desktop onion search aggregator. Ghost is the mobile extension of this research toolchain.

The app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect any data from anyone, including children. The app contains no content designed for minors. Search results come from public onion search engines and may contain mature content — the 18+ consent gate and content rating reflect this.
